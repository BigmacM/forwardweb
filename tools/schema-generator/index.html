How do you add LocalBusiness schema to your site?

Fill in your details and get JSON-LD you can paste into your site, checked as you type.

Schema generatorRuns in your browser

This generator needs JavaScript. It builds the JSON-LD in your browser from the details you type.

What to describe

Your JSON-LD

Add a name and a website address to see your code.

What this checks
That the fields a machine needs are present and well formed: name, URL, phone, address, and a sensible description. It then writes the JSON-LD for you.
The gotcha
Structured data helps machines confirm who you are, but it does not guarantee a rich result or a recommendation. It only has to match what is visible on the page.
What it can't see
It does not test your live page or run Google's Rich Results Test. After you paste the code in, check the page with Google's own validator.
QuestionsShort answers.
Where do I paste the code?
Inside a script tag of type application/ld+json in the head or body of the page it describes. Most site builders have a place for custom code.
Does schema make me rank higher?
Not directly. It helps search engines and assistants understand and trust the facts on your page. That is useful, but it is not a ranking switch.
Is anything sent to a server?
No. The page builds the JSON in your browser and nothing you type leaves it.
