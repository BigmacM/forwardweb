Does an AI assistant know your business exists?

Eight questions about your site and listings. You get a score and the three fixes I would make first, written the way I would say them.

AI visibility checkQuestion 1 of 8

This check needs JavaScript. Here are the eight questions, so you can work through them on paper. Score two for yes, one for partly, none for no or not sure.

  1. Does your homepage say what you do, where, and for whom in the first paragraph?
  2. Are AI crawlers allowed in your robots.txt?
  3. Do your pages carry structured data for your business, services and FAQs?
  4. Is your Google Business Profile claimed, complete and recently reviewed?
  5. Do your key pages answer buyer questions directly: price, process, timing?
  6. Do you publish with a named author and a date?
  7. Is your name, address and phone identical on every directory?
  8. Can you see which visitors arrive from AI assistants?

12 or more is a strong base. 6 to 11 is findable, not yet recommended. 5 or fewer, AI assistants cannot see you yet. Request the audit for the written version.

    Request the audit

    A self-check, not an audit. It sees what you tell it, not what I would find from outside.

    What this checks

    Whether your homepage opener, crawler access, structured data, Google Business Profile, direct answers, authorship, listings and measurement are in place. Each one is something assistants lean on when they decide who to name.

    The gotcha

    It scores what you tell it. If you answer "not sure", it counts as no, because a thing you cannot confirm is a thing an assistant cannot confirm either.

    What it can't see

    It is a self-check, not an audit. It cannot see your site, ask an assistant about you, or find the problems you do not know to look for.

    QuestionsShort answers.

    Is my answer sent anywhere?

    No. The check runs entirely in your browser. If you then request the audit, your score is added to that form so I can see where you start, and only if you choose to send it.

    What does the score mean?

    Twelve or more out of sixteen is a strong base. Six to eleven means assistants can probably find you but have little reason to recommend you. Five or fewer means they likely cannot see you yet.

    Does a high score mean ChatGPT will recommend me?

    No. Nothing guarantees that. A high score means you have done the groundwork assistants rely on.
