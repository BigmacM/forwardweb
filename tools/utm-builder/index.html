How do you build consistent UTM links?

Consistent campaign links, with a guard so Facebook and facebook stop splitting your reports.

UTM link builderRuns in your browser

This builder needs JavaScript. A UTM link is your page address plus ?utm_source=...&utm_medium=...&utm_campaign=..., all lowercase with no spaces.

Your link

Add a page address, a source, a medium and a campaign.
What this checks
It builds a tagged URL from your parts and flags the naming mistakes that fragment analytics: mixed case, spaces and parameters in the wrong place.
The gotcha
Consistency matters more than the tags themselves. Analytics treats "Facebook" and "facebook" as two different sources, so pick a convention and keep it.
What it can't see
It does not track anything or store your links. It only builds text, so keep your own list of the names you have used.
QuestionsShort answers.
Which UTM parameters are required?
Source, medium and campaign are the usual three. Term and content are optional, for paid keywords and for telling similar links apart.
Should I tag my internal links?
No. Tagging links inside your own site starts a new session and overwrites where the visitor really came from.
Why lowercase?
Because reports are case sensitive. One lowercase convention keeps the same channel in one row.
