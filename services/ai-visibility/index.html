The plain answerWhat it is, who it is for, and what it costs.

People now ask an assistant who to hire before they ever open a search result. An AI visibility audit checks whether your business is part of that answer, and if it isn't, why not.

It is for businesses that win customers on trust and local knowledge: trades, clinics, studios, tour operators, professional practices. It is not a promise that an assistant will recommend you. Nobody can sell you that. It is a written list of what is in your control, in the order I would fix it.

The audit is a fixed price, and you keep the written plan whether or not we work together.

QuestionsThe ones I get asked most.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and be wary of anyone who says they can. Assistants weigh sources differently and change over time. I fix what is in your control, check the result with repeated real questions, and tell you what moved.

What do I get at the end?

A written plan in priority order, with what to fix, why it matters and what it costs to fix. It is yours to keep. You can hand it to your own developer or ask me to do the work.

Do I have to hire you to do the fixes?

No. The audit stands on its own. If you want me to do the fixes, I quote them separately and fixed, before any work starts.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO is about ranking in search results. This is about being read and named in an assistant answer. They share a lot of groundwork, such as clean pages and structured data, but the checks are different.
