The plain answerWhat it is, who it is for, and what it costs.

SEO is the ongoing work of making a site easy to find, easy to trust and easy to act on. For a small business that means three layers: the technical base, content that answers what buyers ask, and the local signals Google uses for nearby searches.

I work on a monthly retainer, and I measure it against enquiries and bookings rather than ranking screenshots. Where something did not work, the report says so.

I built and grew my own booking site this way, and I run the search and measurement on it today. That is the work I would be doing for you.

QuestionsThe ones I get asked most.

How long until I see results?

It depends on where the site starts, how competitive the search is and how much needs fixing. I will not promise a date. Measurement goes in first so you can see the direction of travel from the first month.

Why a monthly retainer?

SEO is steady work, not a switch. A retainer keeps content, fixes and reporting moving. If you would rather start with a single deliverable, begin with the audit.

Do you build links?

I do outreach that a human would be happy to receive, and I do not buy links. Links are a small part of the work for most local businesses.

Can I start with the audit?

Yes, and most people should. The audit gives you a written plan and tells us both whether a retainer is worth it.
