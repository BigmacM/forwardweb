The plain answerWhat it is, who it is for, and what it costs.

When a business outgrows spreadsheets, off-the-shelf software often fits badly: you pay for features you do not use and work around the ones you need. I build the smaller thing that fits.

I am building two products of this kind right now, a booking platform for studios and a dispatch system for tour operators, and you can try small demos of both on the In development page. They are private products, and I don't name the businesses.

Web apps start from $12,000 CAD plus HST. The quote is fixed once we have agreed what version one does.

  2. DispatchAssign work to people and vehicles with conflicts caught before they happen.
  3. Back officeSimple internal tools for the jobs your team does by hand today.

QuestionsThe ones I get asked most.

Why from $12,000?

Because software that handles bookings, payments or dispatch has to be right. The price covers design, build, testing and handover for a first version that does one job properly.

Do I own the software?

Yes. The code and the accounts are yours, whether or not we keep working together.

Can you integrate with the tools I already use?

Often, yes. It depends on whether those tools have an API. I check that before I quote.

Can I see something first?

You can try the two demos on the In development page. They are small pieces of the real logic, and nothing in them is saved.

Tell me what you're trying to fix.

Taking projects now, a few at a time, so I read every request. You'll hear back within two working days, with a yes, a no, or a better idea.
