If someone types your business name into Google, what do they see beside the results? Until recently that was often a Knowledge Panel built from your Google Business Profile and a few other sources. For some brand searches it is now an AI Overview.

I am writing this a few days after the report, so what follows is what is known, what is not, and a short check you can run today.

## What changed in Google's brand results?

On 2 November 2026, Barry Schwartz at Search Engine Roundtable [reported that Google had moved AI Overviews for many brand-related searches to the right-hand column](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-brands-right-42440.html) of the results page. They previously appeared near the top or in the middle. The report credits SEO practitioners who spotted the change on X and shared screenshots of brand searches, including large consumer brands, where the AI Overview sits in the panel position.

A few limits are worth stating plainly. I checked the article on 3 November 2026. Google has not announced the change in anything I can find. The evidence is screenshots and posts, and the article describes a layout on desktop in some cases, with variation. It may not look the same for your searches, in your city or on your device. And the author's own view is a mixed one: he likes the placement, and he is wary of an AI describing a brand as accurately as the brand's own site would.

## Why does it matter for a small business?

Your name is the search people run when they already want you. A customer was referred, saw your van or heard your name, and now checks you out. What appears beside the results at that moment is part of how they decide.

If Google writes that description from whatever it can find, it will reflect the quality of what it finds. An out-of-date address on an old directory, a closed branch, a previous name, or a service you stopped offering can all end up in a sentence you never approved.

This is not a new risk. It is the same one I described in [How to check whether ChatGPT recommends your business](/notes/check-chatgpt-recommends-your-business/): when your facts conflict across the web, assistants hedge, get details wrong or name someone else. What has changed is how visible the result is when a person searches your name.

## The 10-minute check

You do not need a tool for the first half. Do it in a private window so your search history does not colour the result.

<div class="method">

1. **Search your exact business name.** Note what appears in the right-hand column, if anything. Take a screenshot with the date on it.
2. **Search the name plus your city.** Do it again, since local results often differ. Screenshot that too.
3. **Read every line against your real facts.** Check the address, phone, hours, services, founding details and anything described as "known for". Mark each line right, wrong or missing.
4. **Click any source it cites.** Write down which sites it drew on. Those are the sites shaping what Google says about you.
5. **Fix the wrong lines at the source.** Edit your Google Business Profile first, then the directory or page the AI cited. Do not try to correct the summary itself. Fix the facts underneath it.

</div>

That takes about ten minutes. Keep the screenshots, because they give you a dated record to compare against next month.

## What should you not do?

Do not rush to rewrite your homepage because of one layout test. Google can reverse or reshape this next week. The reliable response is the unglamorous one: accurate facts everywhere, a clear opener, and a profile you control. ## FAQ

### Does my small business even get an AI Overview for its name?

I do not know, and neither does anyone else yet. The reports cover well-known brands and some brand-related searches, not local businesses in general. Run the check and see.

### Can I edit what the AI Overview says?

Not directly. Fix the source facts, mainly your Google Business Profile and your own site, then recheck. Changes can take time to show up.

### Is this the same as my Knowledge Panel?

No. A Knowledge Panel is built from structured sources. An AI Overview is generated text. The report describes AI Overviews appearing where a Knowledge Panel used to be for some searches, not Google removing Knowledge Panels everywhere.
